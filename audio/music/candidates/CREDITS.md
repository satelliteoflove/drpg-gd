# Music candidate pool — provenance & licensing

Debug A/B audition pool for the dungeon biomes (and bonus town/combat slots).
Audition in-game with the jukebox: **Shift+.** = next, **Shift+,** = previous
(the `>` / `<` keys; cycles every file here; on-screen label shows the current track).

**All tracks are CC0 / public domain** — no attribution or share-alike is legally
required. Provenance is recorded anyway for traceability. Three tracks were
multi-licensed on OpenGameArt with CC0 offered as one option; we elect CC0.

Once you pick the winners I'll move the chosen files into `audio/music/`, wire
them into `FloorThemes`, and trim this pool (it should not ship as-is).
